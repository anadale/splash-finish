# Splash Finish 0.3.15

- Snooze now keeps the panel hidden for its whole time. New events still land in the
  inbox and update the menu bar count; the panel opens with everything collected
  meanwhile when the snooze ends, when you show the inbox (Show Inbox, `⌥⌘S`,
  `splash-finish show`), or when the inbox becomes empty. Before, any new event
  ended the snooze.
- ⌘C and ⌘V now work in pi question fields.
- Splash Finish installs in `/Applications`, like the other bobuk.io apps. The
  standalone installer replaces and removes a copy that older installers put in
  `~/Applications`; without administrator rights, pass `--app-dir ~/Applications`.
- The inbox panel, Settings, the `⌥⌘S` hotkey, Start at Login and the update flow now
  come from BobukKit 0.2.1, the shared bobuk.io component library.

Your inbox, settings and agent integrations are retained. Homebrew copies update with
**Update Splash Finish via Homebrew…**; standalone copies update with **Check for Updates…**.
